# ✅ Arrow Pad Now Works for Existing Animals!

## 🎯 **WHAT CHANGED:**

The **arrow pad for precision placement** now works for **ALL animals on your tree**, not just newly unlocked ones!

---

## 🎮 **HOW TO USE (For Existing Animals):**

### **Step 1: Long Press**
- **Long press (0.5 seconds)** on any animal already on your tree
- Animal gets a **blue circle** around it (selected)
- **Green checkmark** button appears

### **Step 2: Position with Arrow Pad**
- **Arrow pad automatically appears** at the bottom of the screen
- **⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️ Tap arrows** to move 10px at a time
- **Or drag** the animal for rough positioning
- Haptic feedback on every move

### **Step 3: Confirm**
- Tap the **green "Done"** button on the arrow pad
- **OR** tap the **green checkmark** on the animal itself
- Animal is now precisely positioned! ✅

### **💡 Bonus: Flip Orientation**
- **Quick tap** (not long press) on any animal to **flip it horizontally** ↔️
- Great for making animals face different directions!
- Works anytime (not just in edit mode)

---

## 🆕 **NEW vs OLD:**

### **Before:**
- ❌ Long press → Drag only → Auto-exit
- ❌ No precision control
- ❌ Hard to place exactly where you want

### **After:**
- ✅ Long press → **Arrow pad appears**
- ✅ Drag for rough position
- ✅ **Arrows for 10px precision**
- ✅ **Green "Done" button** to confirm
- ✅ Haptic feedback
