```

exclusive discounts that make financial wellness more accessible.

qualified customer referrals from a trusted source.

Founder, Soteria
frank@montebay.io
```

## ✅ **QUICK CHECKLIST - Before Sending**

For every outreach email, make sure:
- [ ] Partner name is spelled correctly
- [ ] Contact name is spelled correctly (check LinkedIn)
- [ ] Email is personalized (mention something specific about their company)
- [ ] Value proposition is clear
- [ ] Call-to-action is obvious
- [ ] Signature has all contact info
- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors
- [ ] You've researched the company (5 min minimum)
- [ ] You know what discount you'd want from them
- [ ] You can answer "why this partner?" if they ask

---

## 🎯 **CUSTOMIZATION TIPS**

### **For Financial Services Partners:**
Emphasize mission alignment, financial wellness, member demographics

### **For Retail Partners:**
Emphasize qualified traffic, conversion potential, engaged audience

### **For Local Businesses:**
Emphasize community connection, local support, in-person opportunities

### **For Startups:**
Emphasize growth opportunity together, flexibility, mutual support

### **For Enterprise:**
Emphasize professional setup, data/reporting, scale potential

## 🎯 **DAILY OUTREACH WORKFLOW**

**Morning (30 min):**
1. Research 5 new partners (10 min)
2. Find contact info (10 min)
3. Write 5 personalized emails (10 min)

**Mid-Day (15 min):**
1. Send the 5 emails
2. Update tracking spreadsheet
3. Respond to any replies

**Afternoon (20 min):**
1. Follow up on emails sent 4-7 days ago
2. LinkedIn connects for new prospects
3. Prep for any scheduled calls

**Weekly (1 hour):**
1. Review tracking spreadsheet
2. Analyze response rates
3. Adjust messaging if needed
4. Plan next week's targets

---

**Pro Tip:** Aim for consistency over volume. 3-5 quality, personalized emails 
per day is better than 20 generic ones. Build relationships, not just deals! 🚀
