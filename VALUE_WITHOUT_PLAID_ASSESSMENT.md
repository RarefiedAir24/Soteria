## ⚠️ **HONEST CHALLENGES**

### **Friction Points:**

#### **1. Manual Entry Fatigue** 😩
- User makes deposit → Must open app → Enter amount
- User forgets → Data is incomplete
- After 2-3 weeks: "This is annoying"

**Risk:** 40-50% churn after first month

#### **2. Value Proposition Confusion** 🤔
- "Wait, I'm paying $7/month to track savings MANUALLY?"
- "Why not just use a free spreadsheet?"
- Hard to explain the behavioral value

**Risk:** Low conversion (5-8% vs 10-15% industry standard)

#### **3. Competitive Comparisons** 📊

**Users will compare to:**
- Qapital: $3/month WITH bank sync
- Digit: $5/month WITH bank sync
- You: $6.99/month WITHOUT bank sync

**Perception:** "More expensive with less features?"

**Risk:** Negative reviews, low conversions
