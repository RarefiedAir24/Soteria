## Executive Summary

### ✅ **VERDICT: MONETIZABLE, BUT WITH CRITICAL GAPS**

**Current State**: The app has a solid foundation for monetization, but several premium features are either missing or not properly gated, which dilutes the value proposition.

**Key Findings**:
- ✅ Pricing is competitive and fair
- ✅ Core value proposition is unique (behavioral savings vs budgeting)
- ⚠️ Premium features are incomplete (Power Actions missing, AI not gated)
- ⚠️ Free tier may be too generous (reduces upgrade urgency)
- ⚠️ Value communication needs improvement

**Recommendation**: **Fix gaps before launch** - Complete premium features and gate AI to justify pricing.

## 9. Critical Recommendations

### Priority 1: Fix Before Launch (CRITICAL)

1. **Gate AI Features** ⚠️ **HIGH PRIORITY**
   - Make AI suggestions premium-only
   - Show locked preview for free users
   - **Impact**: Restores premium value, justifies pricing
   - **Effort**: Low (add premium checks)
   - **Timeline**: 1-2 days

2. **Enforce Decision Windows Daily Limit** ⚠️ **HIGH PRIORITY**
   - Free: Max 1 Decision Window per day
   - Premium: Up to 3 Decision Windows per day
   - **Impact**: Creates upgrade trigger, balances free tier
   - **Effort**: Medium (add daily limit logic)
   - **Timeline**: 2-3 days

3. **Gate Goal Impact Feature** ⚠️ **MEDIUM PRIORITY**
   - Move enhanced Goal Impact to premium
   - Free: Basic goal display only
   - Premium: Full impact calculation, regret reminders, dynamic suggestions
   - **Impact**: Adds high-value premium feature
   - **Effort**: Medium (split implementation)
   - **Timeline**: 2-3 days

4. **Implement Power Actions** ⚠️ **MEDIUM PRIORITY**
