# Soteria Pricing & Value Analysis

## Current Pricing
- **Monthly**: $4.99/month
- **Annual**: $39.99/year (33% discount = $3.33/month effective)
- **Annual Savings**: $20/year vs monthly

## Market Comparison

### Similar Apps Pricing (2024-2025)
1. **YNAB (You Need A Budget)**: $14.99/month or $99/year
   - Full budgeting app with bank sync
   - More comprehensive than Soteria

2. **Mint Alternatives** (Rocket Money, Copilot): $5-10/month
   - Budgeting and expense tracking
   - Bank account aggregation

3. **Digital Wellness Apps** (Freedom, Cold Turkey): $3-7/month
   - App blocking and focus tools
   - Similar to Quiet Hours feature

4. **Savings/Behavioral Apps** (Qapital, Digit): $3-5/month
   - Automated savings
   - Behavioral nudges

### Competitive Position
✅ **$4.99/month is COMPETITIVE** - Lower than most budgeting apps, similar to savings/wellness apps
✅ **$39.99/year is STRONG** - 33% discount is industry standard, effective $3.33/month is excellent value
