### **$9.99 is Only Justified IF:**

1. ✅ You have 10+ quality partners (not just any partners)
2. ✅ Partners offer meaningful discounts (not 2% off something nobody buys)
3. ✅ Discounts are on things people ACTUALLY purchase regularly
