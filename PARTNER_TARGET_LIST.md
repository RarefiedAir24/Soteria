## 💡 **QUICK TIPS**

**Finding Contacts:**
1. Check partner website for "Partnerships" or "Business Development" page
2. Search LinkedIn: "[Company] Partnerships Manager"
3. Try email patterns: partnerships@, bizdev@, marketing@ [company].com
4. Call main number and ask for partnerships team
5. Check Crunchbase or AngelList for startup contacts

**Email Best Practices:**
- ✅ Personalize every email (mention something specific)
- ✅ Lead with their benefit, not yours
- ✅ Keep it under 150 words
- ✅ Clear call-to-action
- ✅ Professional signature

**Increasing Response Rates:**
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am (best open rates)
- Follow up 3-4 days later (80% of deals need 5+ touchpoints)
- Try different channels (email, LinkedIn, phone)
- Leverage warm intros when possible
