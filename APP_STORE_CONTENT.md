## 📝 App Description (4000 characters max)

```
Soteria helps you build better financial habits through intentional moments of pause. Watch your savings grow with a beautiful, visual money tree that represents your goals and milestones.

🌳 GROW YOUR MONEY TREE
See your savings come to life with a dynamic, animated money tree. Each leaf represents a milestone or goal, and as you save, your tree grows and flourishes. The tree adapts to your progress, with leaves filling in as you reach your savings targets.

💰 GOAL-BASED SAVINGS
Set meaningful savings goals with photos, target dates, and progress tracking. Whether you're saving for a vacation, a new gadget, or a special purchase, Soteria helps you visualize your progress and stay motivated. Create goals from product links—just paste a URL from any e-commerce site and Soteria will extract the price and help you plan your savings.

⏰ DECISION WINDOWS
Set intentional moments to pause before spending. Decision Windows are time-based prompts that help you make conscious savings decisions. When a window opens, choose to save, protect your money, or simply pause and reflect. It's your moment to decide—before the impulse takes over.

🛡️ PROTECTION HOURS
Get gentle reminders during times when you're most likely to make impulse purchases. Set Protection Hours for specific days and times, and Soteria will send you thoughtful notifications to help you pause and consider your choices.

🤝 SHARED GOALS
Invite friends and family to save together for shared purchases. Whether it's a group vacation, a gift, or a shared expense, collaborate on savings goals and track everyone's contributions in real-time.

🎯 MICRO-COMMITMENTS
Build daily and weekly savings habits with streak tracking. Set small, achievable commitments and watch your streak grow. Celebrate your wins and build momentum toward your larger financial goals.

🏦 DEDICATED SAVINGS ACCOUNTS
Create a dedicated, FDIC-insured savings account for all your goals. Keep your savings separate from your everyday spending, with easy transfers via Plaid or account credentials.

✨ KEY FEATURES

• Beautiful Money Tree Visualization
  Watch your savings grow with an animated tree that adapts to your progress

• Smart Goal Creation
  Paste any product or cart URL and Soteria extracts the price automatically

• Decision Windows
  Intentional moments to pause and make conscious savings choices

• Protection Hours
  Gentle reminders during vulnerable spending times

• Deposit Tracking
  Complete history of all your savings with daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly views

• Micro-Commitments & Streaks
  Build habits with daily/weekly commitments and track your progress

• Shared Goals
  Collaborate with others on group savings goals

• AI-Powered Insights (Premium)
  Personalized savings suggestions and timing recommendations

• Privacy-First
  Your financial data is secure and private. We never judge, only support.

Soteria is designed to be non-judgmental, privacy-first, and focused on helping you make better financial decisions—one moment at a time. No shaming, no guilt, just gentle support as you build healthier financial habits.

Start growing your money tree today.
```
