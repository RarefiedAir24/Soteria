# How to Edit Decision Window Alarm Time

**Status**: ✅ Notification received at 3:01pm - System working!

**Next**: Edit the alarm to 2:50pm

---

## 📝 How to Edit the Alarm

### Step 1: Open Decision Windows
1. **Open Soteria app**
2. **Navigate to Decision Windows**:
   - Go to **Settings** → **Decision Windows**, OR
   - Go to **Home** → **Decision Windows** section

### Step 2: Find Your Window
1. **Look for**: "My Decision Notification"
2. **Tap** on it to edit

### Step 3: Edit the Time
1. **Change time** from **3:01pm** to **2:50pm**
2. **Save** the changes
3. This will **reschedule** all notifications

---

## ✅ What Happens When You Edit

When you save the edited window:
- ✅ **Old notifications** are cancelled
- ✅ **New notifications** are scheduled for 2:50pm
- ✅ **Today's notification** rescheduled (if before 2:50pm)
- ✅ **Recurring notifications** updated for new time

---

## 🎯 Expected Result

After editing to 2:50pm:
- ✅ Notification will fire at **2:50pm** (not 3:01pm)
- ✅ Recurring notifications updated to 2:50pm
- ✅ All days (2, 3, 5, 6) will use new time

---
