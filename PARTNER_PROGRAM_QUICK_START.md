## 🚨 **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

### **1. Not Personalizing Emails**
❌ Generic mass emails get ignored
✅ Spend 2-3 min per email to personalize

### **2. Giving Up After One Email**
❌ Most people need 3-5 touchpoints
✅ Follow up 3-4 times over 2 weeks

### **3. Targeting Too Many Partners at Once**
❌ Spreading yourself too thin
✅ Focus on 5-10 quality conversations

### **4. Not Tracking Outreach**
❌ Forgetting who you contacted
✅ Use spreadsheet religiously

### **5. Not Following Up**
❌ Leaving deals on the table
✅ Set reminders to follow up

## 📞 **Need Help? Common Questions**

**Q: What if I don't have any users yet?**
A: Start with "growing community" or "TestFlight beta users." Even 50-100 engaged users is enough for local businesses or startups.

**Q: What if partners ask about user numbers?**
A: Be honest. Emphasize quality over quantity: "We're in beta with [X] highly-engaged savers who check the app daily and are actively working toward financial goals."

**Q: What discount should I ask for?**
A: Start with 10-15% for retail/services, $10-50 off for one-time purchases, 1-3 months free for subscriptions. Be flexible!

**Q: What if they say no?**
A: Ask why, learn from feedback, thank them, move on. "No" today might be "yes" in 6 months when you have more traction.

**Q: Do I need a lawyer for partnership agreements?**
A: For first few partners, a simple agreement is fine (use template in strategy doc). For larger partners or complex terms, yes, consult a lawyer.

**Q: How do I track if members are using discounts?**
A: Unique discount codes (count redemptions) or affiliate links (track clicks/conversions). Most partners can provide this data.
