# App Icon Resolution Settings

**Question**: What resolution (DPI/PPI) should I use when saving 1024x1024?

**Answer**: For digital/screen use, **resolution doesn't matter**, but use **72 DPI** or **144 DPI** (either works)

---

## 📐 Resolution Settings

### Recommended:
- **72 DPI** (standard for web/digital)
- **144 DPI** (also fine, slightly higher quality)
- **300 DPI** (overkill for digital, but won't hurt)

### What Matters:
- ✅ **Pixel dimensions**: 1024×1024 pixels (this is what matters!)
- ⚠️ **DPI/PPI**: Doesn't affect the final image for digital use

---

## 🎯 Why Resolution Doesn't Matter

**For digital/screen images**:
- iOS displays images by **pixel dimensions**, not DPI
- A 1024×1024 image at 72 DPI = same as 1024×1024 at 300 DPI
- DPI only matters for **print** (physical media)

**What iOS cares about**:
- ✅ Pixel width: 1024
- ✅ Pixel height: 1024
- ❌ DPI setting: Ignored

---

## 💡 Practical Answer

### When Your Design Tool Asks:

**Option 1: Use 72 DPI** (Most Common)
- Standard for digital/screen
- Most design tools default to this
- Perfectly fine for app icons

**Option 2: Use 144 DPI** (Also Fine)
- Slightly higher, but same result
- Some tools default to this
- Works great

**Option 3: Use 300 DPI** (Overkill, but OK)
- Higher than needed for digital
- Won't cause problems
- Just makes file slightly larger (unnecessarily)

---

## ✅ Recommended Settings

### Export Settings:
- **Width**: 1024 pixels
- **Height**: 1024 pixels
- **Resolution**: **72 DPI** (or 144 DPI)
- **Format**: PNG
- **Color Mode**: RGB
- **No Transparency**: Opaque

---

## 🎨 Common Design Tools

### Photoshop:
- **Image Size**: 1024×1024 pixels
- **Resolution**: 72 pixels/inch (or 144)
- **Export**: PNG, RGB, No Transparency

### Figma:
- **Frame Size**: 1024×1024
- **Export**: PNG, 1x (100%)
- Resolution handled automatically

### Sketch:
- **Artboard**: 1024×1024
- **Export**: PNG, 1x
- Resolution handled automatically

### Preview (Mac):
- **Tools** → **Adjust Size**: 1024×1024 pixels
- Resolution: 72 DPI (default)

---

## 📋 Quick Answer

**When asked for resolution, choose**:
- ✅ **72 DPI** (recommended)
- ✅ **144 DPI** (also fine)

**Both will produce the exact same result** - a 1024×1024 pixel image that iOS will accept!

---

## ⚠️ Important

**What matters**:
- ✅ 1024×1024 **pixels**
- ✅ PNG format
- ✅ No transparency

**What doesn't matter**:
- ❌ DPI/PPI setting (for digital use)
- ❌ File size (within reason)
- ❌ Color profile (sRGB is fine)

---

**Bottom Line**: Use **72 DPI** or **144 DPI** - either works perfectly! The pixel dimensions (1024×1024) are what matter.
