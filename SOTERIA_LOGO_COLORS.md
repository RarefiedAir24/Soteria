## 🎨 Design Tips

### Logo Composition:
- **Background**: Use one color (light or dark) as solid background
- **Logo**: Use the other color for the logo mark/text
- **Contrast**: Ensure good contrast for visibility at 60x60

### Examples:
1. **Dark background** (`midnightSlate`) + **Light logo** (`softGraphite`)
2. **Light background** (`softGraphite`) + **Dark logo** (`midnightSlate`)
3. **Gradient**: Blend from `softGraphite` to `midnightSlate`
