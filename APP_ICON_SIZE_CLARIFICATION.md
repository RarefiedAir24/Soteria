# App Icon Size - 1024x1024 at 100%

**Question**: Should the app icon be 1024x1024 at 100%?

**Answer**: ✅ **YES - Exactly 1024x1024 pixels at 100% (1x resolution)**

---

## 📐 Exact Specifications

### Size:
- **Width**: 1024 pixels
- **Height**: 1024 pixels
- **Resolution**: 100% (1x) - NOT 2x or 3x
- **Total pixels**: 1,048,576 pixels

### Format:
- **PNG format**
- **No transparency** (opaque background)
- **RGB color mode** (not RGBA)

---

## ✅ What "100%" Means

**100% = 1x resolution**:
- This is the **base resolution**
- NOT 2x (@2x) or 3x (@3x)
- Just a standard 1024x1024 pixel image
- No scaling needed

**iOS automatically generates**:
- 60x60 (@2x = 120x120) for iPhone
- 60x60 (@3x = 180x180) for iPhone Plus
- 76x76 (@2x = 152x152) for iPad
- And all other required sizes

**You only provide ONE image**: 1024x1024 at 1x

## 🎨 Design at 100% Scale

### When Designing:
- Design at **actual size**: 1024x1024 pixels

- Export at **100% scale** (not 200% or 300%)

### Export Settings:
- **Width**: 1024 px
- **Height**: 1024 px
- **Resolution**: 72 DPI or 144 DPI (doesn't matter for digital)
- **Scale**: 100% (1x)

## ❌ Common Mistakes

### Don't Do:
- ❌ Create 2048x2048 and scale down (unnecessary)
- ❌ Create 512x512 and scale up (will be blurry)
- ❌ Export at 2x or 3x resolution
- ❌ Use @2x or @3x naming

### Do:
- ✅ Create exactly 1024x1024 pixels
- ✅ Export at 100% scale
- ✅ Use standard PNG format
- ✅ No special naming needed (just the file)
